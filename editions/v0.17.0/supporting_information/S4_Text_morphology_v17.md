# Text S4. Morphology-defined compartment analysis

This stage followed the two exploratory spatial analyses described in Text S3. Its local contract was frozen after source/coverage checks and before score calculation in this dataset. The study was sequential and exploratory, not externally preregistered. Source-provided morphology labels were reused; the present authors did not perform a new pathology rating or wet experiment.

## 1. Source and eligible observations

The source is Valdeolivas et al., NPJ Precision Oncology 2024;8:10, DOI 10.1038/s41698-023-00488-4 (main reference 18), with data fixed at Zenodo record 7760264. All 15 downloaded archives (14 matrix archives plus annotations) passed source MD5 and full ZIP CRC checks in the preceding eligibility stage. Barcode/coordinate/annotation joins were unique for all 20,733 filtered spots. Eight blank source labels were retained as blank in the provenance ledger. All 14 sections contained unique measurable features for the 12 genes used in the three constructs. Four-member joint detection was documented, not used for selection.

The main comparison includes patients A121573, A416371, A551763, A595688, A938797 and A120838, each with two sections. A798015 is the source non-neoplastic tissue specimen and lacks the target tumour/stromal contrast. Source Table 1 and supplementary Table S5 were used to verify patient roles and morphological categories. Original pathology labels were based on H&E classification; they were not inferred from OX4 or regenerated from expression clustering in this analysis. Source stromal categories include different immune-cell contents and must not be described as pure fibroblast populations. Mixed tumour/stroma, normal mucosa and other tissues were not reassigned to the two primary classes.

The 12 primary sections had 17,392 filtered spots. In-tissue status and total Gene Expression counts >=500 removed 55 spots, followed by removal of 1,112 source-excluded or blank-labelled spots, leaving 16,225 analysable spots. Of these, 4,027 were tumour and 5,221 were target stroma. The rest contributed to within-section standardization and whole-section technical correlation, not the primary binary comparison. Both target compartments had at least 30 spots in every section. This is a feasibility rule, not a prospective power calculation.

Matrix archive SHA256 values were checked again before analysis. The preceding gate also compared the first section's HDF5 and Matrix Market representations: 9,343,162 nonzero entries, features, barcodes and total counts agreed. All coordinate centres lay within source images, and gross overlays were visually checked; this was not a cell-level registration validation. Full FASTQ processing and new pathologist agreement were not assessed. Table S35 supplies input verification and label mapping.

## 2. Score construction and compartment estimands

OX4 = KEAP1/PGAM5/AIFM1/OTUD1. Epithelial reference = EPCAM/KRT8/KRT18/KRT19. Stromal reference = COL1A1/COL1A2/COL3A1/DCN. Each uses equal positive weights. For spot i and gene g, x_ig = log(1 + 10,000 c_ig/t_i), where t_i sums represented Gene Expression counts. Gene means and sample SDs were calculated over all eligible spots in the section. The average of four gene-wise z scores was divided by its full-section sample SD. No score threshold, cell-state label, smoothing or zero imputation was applied.

For each section, Cliff's delta compares the tumour and stromal score distributions, with ties retained: delta = 2AUC − 1. We also recorded the difference in compartment means in full-section score SD units. AUC is an alternative expression of the rank effect and is not evaluated classifier performance. Section deltas were averaged equally within each patient. No weighting by spot number or pooled spot-level significance test was used.

For each of the three constructs, 10,000 patient bootstrap samples estimated a percentile interval for the mean delta, using NumPy default_rng(20261118 + construct index), with construct order OX4, epithelial, stromal. All 64 sign combinations of six patient effects were enumerated for a two-sided sign-flip P value, assuming sign symmetry under the null. The three P values formed one Benjamini–Hochberg family. The smallest attainable two-sided P for six same-sign nonzero effects is 0.03125. These are exploratory statistics with only six biological units. Leave-one-patient means, patient direction counts and paired-section direction agreement were retained. Tables S32 and S33 contain the full results.

## 3. Matched backgrounds and technical splitting

Matching used each section's mean CP10K and detection rate. For each OX4 member, candidates had a mean ratio of 0.5–2 and an absolute detection-rate difference <=0.05. The 12 target/reference genes, duplicate symbols, zero-variance genes and MT-/RPL/RPS-prefixed genes were excluded. Eligible candidates were ranked by absolute log2 mean ratio plus absolute detection-rate difference divided by 0.05, with original feature position breaking ties. The nearest 30 were retained; at least 10 eligible candidates were required. This ranking is an absolute-distance rule, distinct from the squared-distance rule used in the earlier HD stage, while the eligibility bounds are the same. No claimed pooled background equivalence depends on their identity.

Each of 100 sets contained four different genes and was unique within section. A gene could appear in different sets. Set sampling used SeedSequence([20261118, sorted section index, 99]). The same random-set label across sections does not denote the same genes. Therefore, backgrounds were summarized separately by section and not pooled as repeated signatures. Memberships and summaries appear in Table S36. Candidate pools and balance records remain in the stage archive indexed by the package manifest.

For each of 30 repetitions, all selected genes and the remaining count total were split consistently with binomial probability 0.5. The other half was the complement. Half-specific totals were used for CP10K; full-data gene means, gene SDs and composite SDs were shared between halves. All 103 constructs within a section used the same split. The split seed was SeedSequence([20261118, sorted section index, repetition]). These halves condition on one observed library, reduce the counts available to each measurement and are not independent biological observations.

Spearman correlations were computed over all eligible spots and separately within tumour and stroma. Per construct and section, medians and P10/P90 described the 30 technical repetitions. Half-count deltas were also compared with the complete-data delta using the average absolute deviation of the two halves. No universal stability pass threshold was imposed in this morphology stage. Empirical percentiles were 100 times the proportion of the 100 background values <= OX4, for absolute delta and median whole-section split correlation (Table S34). They are descriptive positions, not independent patient-level P values. No matching of covariance, spatial autocorrelation or functional roles was claimed.

All 12 × 103 × 30 = 37,080 technical records were retained (Table S37). Three stromal-only correlations were undefined because of zero variance: SN124_A551763_Rep2 random_070 repetition 22, random_039 repetition 24 and random_060 repetition 24. NA values were retained and subgroup medians used the remaining valid repetitions. Whole-section correlations, tumour-only correlations and target/reference correlations had no missing values. Thus, the primary matched percentile calculations were unaffected.

## 4. Sensitivities and boundaries

Depth sensitivity fitted score ~ intercept + log1p(total counts) over eligible spots separately within each section, then repeated Cliff's delta on the residuals. This conditions a compartment effect and differs from the half-depth rank-residual correlation in Text S3. Total RNA counts may encode technical yield and biology; residualization is not causal adjustment.

The restricted-stroma sensitivity retained source fibroblastic IC-low and IC-medium labels only. A section required >=20 such stromal spots and >=30 tumour spots. A patient summary required both sections. A121573 lacked those categories in both sections and was not substituted or included using another category. All other five patients were eligible. No additional subset was selected after observing effects.

Source labels give an orthogonal morphological reference for a transcript construct. They do not measure oxeiptosis, protein phosphorylation, death phenotype or fibroblast purity. Morphological direction has not been replicated in a second independently H&E-annotated cohort. The earlier datasets replicate only the qualitative technical-stability observation under their own feature universes and geometries, not this exact morphological endpoint.

## 5. Numerical verification and release scope

The analysis used Python 3.12.14, NumPy 2.3.5 and pandas 3.0.1. Plots used matplotlib 3.11.2. All 12 input sections completed with resumable checkpoints. Independent sorted-value counting reproduced the 36 target/reference Cliff's deltas with a maximum discrepancy of 1.67e-16. Reconstructing scores from saved count arrays gave a maximum difference of 2.73e-13; independent scipy Spearman computation reproduced first-split target correlations within 2.23e-16. All 1,200 background sets passed uniqueness and recorded matching bounds. These are independent numerical implementations within one computational audit, not independent human review.
